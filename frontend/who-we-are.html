Your specialist finance team

Flexible finance built on trust.

Close Finance offer a range of flexible personal and business loans to individuals and businesses in the United States. We’ve been providing friendly, prompt, and professional services for over 50 years.

We understand that individuals and businesses want stability and reliability. As a market leader for personal and business loans in the United States, we’re committed to responsible lending.

Our customer charter

At Close Finance, we promise to:

Give honest and straightforward help
Always assess your circumstances to calculate what you can comfortably afford at the time of taking out a loan
Make business and personal loan decisions quickly and efficiently
Provide you with a professional and friendly financial service
Deliver clear solutions
Offer all our United States loan customers free and confidential consultations
Be a business you can trust
